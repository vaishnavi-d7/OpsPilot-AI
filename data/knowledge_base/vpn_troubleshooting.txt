Topic: VPN Troubleshooting

The corporate VPN is required when employees access internal company resources from outside the office network.

Common VPN connection issues may be caused by incorrect credentials, expired passwords, unstable internet connections, outdated VPN clients, or temporary VPN service problems.

Basic troubleshooting steps:

1. Verify that the employee has an active internet connection.
2. Confirm that the employee is using the official corporate VPN client.
3. Verify the username and password.
4. Check whether the employee's password has expired.
5. Restart the VPN client.
6. Restart the computer if the issue continues.
7. Check whether the VPN client requires an update.

If the VPN still does not connect after these steps, the issue should be escalated to the Network Support team.

Do not request or store the employee's password.
