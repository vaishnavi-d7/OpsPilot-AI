TECHNOVA IT SUPPORT RUNBOOK
Topic: Corporate Email Troubleshooting

Employees who cannot send or receive corporate email should first verify their internet connection.

Basic troubleshooting steps:

1. Verify internet connectivity.
2. Check whether the corporate email service is experiencing a known outage.
3. Restart the email application.
4. Verify that the mailbox is not full.
5. Check whether the employee can access email through the official webmail portal.
6. Restart the computer if necessary.

If webmail works but the desktop email application does not work, the issue should be escalated to the Endpoint Support team.

If both webmail and the desktop application are unavailable, escalate the issue to the Messaging Support team.
