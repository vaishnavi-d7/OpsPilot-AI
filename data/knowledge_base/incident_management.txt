TECHNOVA IT INCIDENT MANAGEMENT POLICY
Topic: Incident Priority and Escalation

Priority P1:
A critical business service is unavailable or a major security incident is suspected.

P1 incidents must be escalated immediately to the appropriate incident response team.

Priority P2:
A significant business function is affected, but a workaround may exist.

P2 incidents should be escalated promptly to the responsible technical team.

Priority P3:
A normal individual user issue with limited business impact.

P3 incidents can normally follow standard Service Desk procedures.

Priority P4:
A low-impact request, information request, or minor issue.

P4 requests can be handled through normal support workflows.

If an issue indicates a possible security incident, it must be escalated to the Security team rather than handled only as a normal technical support request.
