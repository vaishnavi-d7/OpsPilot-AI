TECHNOVA IT SUPPORT POLICY
Topic: Password Reset

Employees can reset their corporate password through the official company password portal.

Employees must never share their current password with IT support staff.

If self-service password reset fails, the employee should contact the Service Desk.

Password reset requests involving account lockout should be verified according to the company's identity verification procedure before the account is unlocked.

Support staff must never ask users to send passwords through email, chat, screenshots, or tickets.
